About ABIRA

Born from a passion for fashion and fitness, and the quiet frustration of never finding truly stylish activewear.

Founded on a gap in the market

ABIRA began during founder Abir’s college years spent training in boutique gyms, where one thing kept standing out: stylish, functional activewear was hard to find.

With a vision to redefine the category, Abir pursued a master’s degree in Fashion and Luxury Brand Management and created ABIRA with a clear purpose, to finally stop settling, and to create activewear that empowers beyond performance.

We believe what you wear while you work out should reflect how you want to feel: confident, comfortable, and empowered. Fitness is not just about movement; it’s a lifestyle, and style deserves to be part of the workout.

Why ABIRA

ABIRA exists to remove the compromise between style and performance, because choosing between the two shouldn’t be a thing anymore.

We create premium activewear that feels effortless to wear and refined in design. Our collections are thoughtfully curated, ensuring quality, exclusivity, and purpose in every piece.

Each piece is defined by fashion-forward details, flattering lines, and refined finishes, allowing you to express who you are through every movement, without compromise.

Abir means the fragrance of flowers: the scent that reaches you before you see the garden.

Our first collection is a garden worn on the body: red roses for confidence, wild poppies for energy, bluebirds for freedom, soft peonies for grace, cherry blossoms for joy, and dragon roses for the strength inside softness.

Every flower tells a different story, but they all come from the same root: the belief that strength and beauty grow together. These pieces are made to move with you through training and everyday life, carrying a little of the garden wherever you go.

Our Purpose

Thoughtfully designed activewear that empowers you to move confidently and express yourself, whether you’re training, running errands, or on the go.

Thoughtful Design

Every ABIRA piece is made with intention. From fabric selection to fit and finishing, we balance performance, comfort, and elevated design so each item feels purposeful, refined, and effortless to wear.

Ethical Production

We work closely with our manufacturing partners to ensure fair working conditions, considered processes, and quality standards rooted in respect for people and craftsmanship.

Giving back

Strength is meant
to be shared.

ABIRA was created for women who move with purpose. One percent of every sale goes toward helping cancer patients access the treatment they need.

Every piece you choose helps someone keep moving forward.
