Privacy

What we collect, which is very little, and what happens to it.

Last updated 27 October 2026

Who we are

ABIRA designs and sells activewear. Our office is at Urb La Viña, Tower A1, floor 3, office 3-3, Valencia, Carabobo, Venezuela. For anything in this policy, write to hello@abiraactive.com.

What this site collects

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, your email address, the subject and your message. Nothing else on this site asks you for information, and there is no account to create.

What this site does not do

This site sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking of any kind. It does not build a profile of you, and it does not sell or rent anyone's details.

Where your message goes

When you send the contact form, the message is passed to Formspree, a form service that forwards it to our inbox. Formspree handles it on our behalf under its own privacy terms. We read it, we reply to it, and we keep it only as long as the conversation needs. Ask us to delete it and we will.

Other companies this page touches

Two, both of them ordinary parts of how a website is served:

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces this page uses. Your browser requests them from Google, which means Google sees your IP address.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and keeps standard server logs, the kind every web server keeps.

Your choices

Email us and we will tell you what we hold from your message, correct it, or delete it. You do not need a reason.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
